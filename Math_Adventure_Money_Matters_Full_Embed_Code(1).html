01 · GET TO KNOW OUR TOPIC

Welcome to Money Matters!

This digital learning platform supports Year 3 pupils in learning addition of money through notes, interactive exploration, real-life shopping simulations and digital assessment.

🎯

Learning outcomes

By the end of the learning activities, pupils will be able to:

  • 1.Add two money values correctly with totals up to RM10,000.
  • 2.Explain how changing one amount affects the sum.
  • 3.Solve real-life shopping problems involving money.
  • 4.Check calculations and learn from feedback.

MALAYSIAN CURRENCY · KNOW YOUR MONEY

🇲🇾 Meet Malaysian Ringgit!

Before you add money, get to know the real Malaysian banknotes and coins. Look carefully at their colours, values and designs.

🌀 Try it! Drag any banknote or coin left and right to turn it around. Use the arrows or “Show back” button to view both sides.
💵 Malaysian banknotes
RM1One ringgit
RM5Five ringgit
RM10Ten ringgit
RM20Twenty ringgit
RM50Fifty ringgit
RM100One hundred ringgit
🪙 Malaysian coins
5 senFive sen
10 senTen sen
20 senTwenty sen
50 senFifty sen
💡 Remember: RM1 = 100 sen. For example, 50 sen + 50 sen = RM1.00. Malaysian 1 sen coins are no longer used in everyday cash payments; cash totals are rounded to the nearest 5 sen. Banknotes and coins shown here are official Bank Negara Malaysia currency images. View BNM currency information ↗
Look closely: The RM1 and RM5 notes are polymer banknotes. Other denominations are cotton-based paper banknotes. Learn to recognise money by its denomination, not only its colour.

STATION 01 · UNDERSTAND

📚 Learn It!

Start with the instructional notes and worked examples. Build your basic understanding before moving to the interactive activities.

🧮

What will you learn?

  • ✓Understand money and its use in everyday situations.
  • ✓Understand the value and position of digits in a number.
  • ✓Arrange money amounts correctly in columns before calculating.
  • ✓Recognise needs and wants and develop basic financial awareness.
✏️

Remember the steps

  1. 1.Write the money values in columns.
  2. 2.Align the correct place values.
  3. 3.Add each column carefully and regroup when needed.
  4. 4.Check your answer.
Worked example
RM1,250 + RM2,375 = RM3,625

Mini Market Adventure

Choose products, read their prices and complete the shopping missions!

📝 Your shopping mission

  • 1.Read the shopping task carefully.
  • 2.Identify the required products.
  • 3.Read and record each product price.
  • 4.Add the prices to calculate the total cost.
  • 5.Choose your answer and check your calculation.

💡 Example calculation

A pupil buys a pencil for RM2.50 and a notebook for RM4.20.

RM2.50 + RM4.20 = RM6.70
Total cost = RM6.70
